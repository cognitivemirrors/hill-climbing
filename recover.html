Recover

your body's signals, alongside your practice

Bring your WHOOP with you

Recover reads your recovery, sleep, and strain from WHOOP so the suite can meet your day where your body actually is. It connects through your own WHOOP developer app — the same bring-your-own-credentials shape as the suite's AI apps.

  1. Sign in at developer.whoop.com and create an app (any name).
  2. Add this exact Redirect URI to the app:
  1. Enable the scopes: read:recovery, read:cycles, read:sleep, read:workout, read:profile, offline.
  2. Copy the Client ID and Client Secret below.

What leaves this device: your WHOOP sign-in and data requests travel from this browser through a small relay on the suite's own Supabase project to WHOOP — the relay stores nothing, logs nothing, and can only reach WHOOP. Your Client ID, Secret, and tokens stay in this browser and are never synced. The health metrics that come back live on this device; if you turn on suite sync they're end-to-end encrypted, so the sync server can't read them either.
