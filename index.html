Before You Begin

Hill Climbing measures your physical stillness through the camera and responds with synthesised sound.

  • If you have a history of seizures, dissociation, panic disorder, PTSD, or severe anxiety, please consult a clinician before regular practice.
  • Stop any time you feel uncomfortable, dizzy, or distressed. Press Esc mid-round to abort with no penalty.
  • This is not therapy or medical care.
  • Watch for back, knee, or neck strain. Take breaks between rounds.
  • Each round caps at 5 minutes. The app will suggest pauses after extended sessions.
