        Cook from your pantry
        Tell the kitchen what you have. A chef writes you a recipe and teaches the craft behind it — the skills you’re building, and how to know you’ve nailed them.
        This is one of the few parts of Hill Climbing that leaves your device (alongside Council and Companion). When you generate a recipe, your pantry list and preferences are sent to Anthropic (Claude) to write it. Nothing else is sent — your progress, your key, and your history stay in this browser. You can remove your key any time.
        Anthropic API key
        Stored only in this browser, and never included in your data export — one key shared across the hub’s AI apps (set it in Council or Companion and it works here too). Get one at console.anthropic.com. Requests bill to your own account — a recipe is a few cents.
