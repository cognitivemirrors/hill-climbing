Leave a wake

The camera watches where you move, and your movement lays down paint. Slow sweeps wash; sharp gestures spark. The painting stays until you clear it — and you can record the making of it as a short film.

Everything runs on your device. No video ever leaves this browser.
A painting made of movement — nothing to win, nothing kept unless you keep it. Frames are compared and thrown away on your machine; only the paint remains, and only until you clear it.
