Turn yourself into an anime frame

Point your webcam at yourself and this draws you as a cel-shaded character in real time — flattened colour, ink outlines, a painted look. Then snapshot or record a clip.

Everything runs on your device. No video ever leaves this browser.
A gentle way to get used to being on camera. Nothing here is uploaded, stored, or analysed — the drawing is made and thrown away frame by frame, on your machine.
