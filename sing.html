Before you sing
One gentle minute first
  1. Three relieved sighs — a soft “haaa” that starts a little high and falls wherever it wants.
  2. A lip trill or a quiet hum, sliding lazily up and down. (If lip trills sputter, “ng” — the end of “sing” — works just as well.)
  3. One slow, quiet siren on “oo” — bottom to top to bottom, smaller and softer at the top, never louder.
Quieter than you think — warm voices climb, forced ones strain. And if anything hurts (hurts, not tires), stop for the day. A voice rebuilds overnight, not mid-session.
